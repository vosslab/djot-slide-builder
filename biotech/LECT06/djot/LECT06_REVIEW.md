## Validation and visual review

Strict Jotdown and repository semantic lint pass for four sources, 256 slides, and 111 image
references. Capacity inspection reports no concerns, including hidden slides. The public
build produces four editable ODP files and four ODP-derived PDFs.

All 218 visible PDF pages were inspected in contact sheets. The original science sequence,
business-plan deck, and copied current A/B decks were also visually inspected. Enlarged
checks covered the two SVG diagrams, revised answer popups, captions, title, and video link.
The first rendered pass found clipped captions, answer paragraphs, and a video heading that
capacity inspection missed. Source wording/layout repairs were rebuilt and reviewed.

Migration judgment: A/B are roughly equivalent visually with current weekly guidance; D is
improved in hierarchy and instructional specificity through comparison tables and worked
examples; C is roughly equivalent overall, with improved diagram labels and image comparisons.
The marked C slides are explicit exceptions awaiting instructor judgment. Small labels baked
into legacy raster figures remain limited by the original artwork resolution.

Native artifact inspection checks authored/hidden/PDF counts, notes, reveal objects, SVG
payloads, and source-slide order. This is not a manual slideshow-click or screen-reader audit.
The full software test suite was not rerun for this content-only conversion.

The Markdown-link check reports 80 passes and four failures in pre-existing documentation
links to older untracked files (`SVG_DIAGRAMS.md`, two follow-up plans, and the Genetics
Lecture 06 review). Those files exist locally but fall outside the check's recent-untracked
allowance. Newly added Biotechnology Lecture 06 links pass. Existing unrelated work was retained.

Generated decks are in `output/odp/` and `output/pdf/`. Local inventories, sample extraction/OCR,
source PDFs, contact sheets, detail renders, and the artifact receipt are in

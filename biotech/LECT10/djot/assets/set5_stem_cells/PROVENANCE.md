Annual student-deck images are retained as visual teaching prompts. Their unverified prose was not treated as source authority; the editable surrounding text was rewritten and checked against the listed research or official sources.

The generic classification poster was removed from Topics 26-28. No topic-specific genomic-QC
or retinal-outcome image was available in those annual source slides; native assay tables,
release workflows, and outcome comparisons replace inappropriate image filler.

The genomic-variation citation is Laurent et al., _Cell Stem Cell_ (2011),
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2010.12.003. The retinal-cell study is Mandai et al., _NEJM_ (2017),
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1608368. Earlier draft links to `nature10135` and `NEJMc1706275`
were incorrect and have been removed from the slides.

Overall Setup of Talking Points Assignment


Talking Points Assigned
Talking points were assigned during last week’s class.
Each student was assigned to one or two talking points based on class size.
Collaboration with Classmates Expectation
Each talking point will have at least two students talking about it.
If you share a talking point with another student: Listen to what they say and do not repeat or duplicate what they said.
Engagement and Points
Keep It Concise: 60 seconds per talking point is short;
Stay on topic and be impactful.
Earn Extra Discussion Marks:
Contributing gets you more discussion points.
If you have something interesting to say about someone else’s talking point, you can score some extra points.
Asking questions of other students gets you discussion marks as well.
Use the chat for the question asking and answering

Formatting Your Talking Point Slide Before Class
Edit and update your slide
Be a Visual Storyteller – limit the amount of text – you do the talking, not your slide.
Images Are Required: Try to have several pictures on your slide to maintain audience attention. You should have some text though.
Add several pictures and some text.
Movies and animated gifs are discouraged but not forbidden.
No external audio is allowed. Movies will be muted.
Limit Text: No complete sentences on slides; use short phrases and keywords only.
Content Preparation
Define the talking point, describe its relation to biotechnology, or provide a recent example of the talking point in biotechnology news.
Try to be unique and creative with your slide’s look and content.
Don’t just copy the book; use the internet for research
Provide your own perspective.
Minimize jargon to make it accessible to all classmates.

Presenting Your Talking Point Slide During Class
Structure
Explain at the level of the class. If you have a technical term, define it. If you do not quite understand it, no one will.
You do not have enough time to cover the entire topic in 60 seconds
so choose the most interesting part or provide outside perspective
but you also need to know the entire topic if I ask questions
Presentation Delivery
Show ENTHUSIASM and make it exciting!
You will unmute to present your talking point, Video is not required
Keep Things to the Point
Do not read directly from slides
put the words you plan to speak in your notes, not the slide.
Put Into Your Own Words
It is okay if you read from notes, but put notes in your own words.
No direct quoting of an article, wikipedia or the textbook
Engagement Tips
Be prepared for questions.
Respect the time limit: If you go over, the audience stops focusing on the talk and starts watching the clock.

# Set 7 topic and source guide

The instructor's Fall 2026 Set #7 list defines all 41 official topics. The
study deck follows that order. Topics 39-41 have no assigned presenters,
so the student deck's Primary Presenters roster ends at topic 38.

This is an instructor synthesis for the requested 12C deck. The 2026 discussion
prompts are the primary question source. Several question sets are adapted for
focus, premise, fit, or safe ethics discussion. Older class material supplies
examples, comparisons, and selected illustrations. Historical student names,
placeholder slides, redundant slides, and dated statistics are absent from the
study deck. Student slide timings and reveals belong to those source decks.

## Instructor source disposition

- Page 1 and page 65 are replaced by the current cover and closer.
- Pages 2-28 are reorganized into ethics, public perception, de-extinction,
  IVF, antibiotics, and plant breeding. The older usage numbers and brand
  commitments are dated. The plant comparisons use editable text.
- Pages 29-39 supply the GloFish example, topic context for biohacking, the
  Wakefield and misconduct anchors, the pig-cloning figure, and the Hwang case.
  The old kit advertisement and pet-cloning pricing are omitted.
- Pages 40-51 supply the Purdue case, the pharma-ethics prompt, and the
  distinction between a cross and a deliberately redesigned animal.
  The Dopesick and mule images are retained. Other movie posters and the
  extended animal-hybrid list are duplicate or supplementary examples.
- Pages 52-60 supply embryonic stem cells, STAP retraction, Dutch elm disease,
  and emerald ash borer. The retraction supports the updated case, while
  the canopy and beetle source images illustrate different tree losses.
- Pages 61-64 contribute the recovery, pathogen oversight, and extra
  controversy themes. The new deck uses documented study outcomes for
  BrainEx and OrganEx and keeps pathogen topics centered on governance,
  public health, and responsible research.

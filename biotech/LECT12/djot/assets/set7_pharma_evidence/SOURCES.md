# Pharmaceutical evidence

- [Naloxone dispensing](naloxone.csv) gives the seven annual national estimates from
  [CDC MMWR, 2019, table 1](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/68/wr/pdfs/mm6831e1-h.pdf).
  IQVIA projected totals from U.S. retail pharmacies. From 2017 the data count prescriptions
  sold to a patient. The line breaks where that definition changed.
- The two county maps reproduce the upper and lower panels of the figure on p. 683 of
  the same CDC report. Both retain the original county colors and legend.
- [Generic prices](generic_prices.csv) transcribes the average manufacturer price panel on
  p. 9 of the [FDA's 2019 analysis](https://www.fda.gov/media/133509/download).
  Each ratio uses the brand price in the three months before generic entry.
  The plot shows medians and the 25th and 75th percentiles.
- The headache example uses invented counts to practice reading an advertisement. The
  Fall 2022 Set 7 slide on drug pricing and advertising (p. 32) inspired this pair.

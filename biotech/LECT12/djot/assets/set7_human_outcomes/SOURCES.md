# Fly brain images

The [whole-brain reconstruction](fly_brain.webp) and [synapse micrograph](fly_synapse.webp)
are panels 1a and 1e of [Dorkenwald et al. (2024)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07558-y/figures/1).
Each panel was cropped from the published figure and encoded as WebP.
The figure is licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The [counts](fly_connectome.csv) come from the same reconstruction.

## Drawing preservation details

- Preserve chromosome arms, centromeres, band addresses, homolog ancestry, copy number and
  segregation outcomes. Keep real karyotypes, molecular reference evidence and empirical plots.
- Preserve direct-repeat and unequal-crossover segment identity and product counts; a simplified
  drawing must not invent a new chromosome mechanism or erase a source intermediate.
- Preserve M2 deletion interval/matrix relationships, every sign and existing gene-placement
  stage. The existing matrix is a resource, not a new table-conversion assignment.
- Preserve source labels and examples in dosage, plant polyploidy, prenatal sampling, inversions,
  translocations and Robertsonian fusion. A source contradiction holds the affected drawing;
  it does not authorize a separate prose/model lesson.
- Great-ape trees (VAR-APE-TREES) are deferred; chromosome-2 fusion remains a mechanism drawing.
  In all mixed data/art cases only the graphic component changes.

## Completion boundary

minimal integration. Do not treat XML validity or source-equivalent contours as art completion. Preserve not-targeted content
and deferred gene trees; unresolved drawing evidence means retain the source with a reason.
Final consumer exports and `source ./source_me.sh && pytest tests/` belong to the existing
plan/ledger. ASCII Djot permits character references; SVG must remain valid XML.

## Findings and limits

- **Material inherited issue outside scoped refresh:** 12C page 5 has a large red `REPLACE ME` overlay obscuring much of the Drosophila/human sex-determination comparison. The pre-existing chromosome marker limitation is acknowledged in the refresh report. It substantially impairs that page and should be repaired before that page is taught; this renumber-only review does not authorize redesign or assert that the 12C deck is ready. Its root source should be fixed and the export rechecked in a separately scoped correction.
- Minor: 11A pages 10, 21, 30-31, 33 and matching 12A pages 10, 21, 33-34, 36 contain relatively dense inherited contact, policy, or registration information. The principal points remain legible; small footer links are better used from the distributed PDF. 12A pages 24-26 are also text-heavy but usable. These do not warrant a redesign in this scope.
- Minor: 11B pages 19-20, 23-24, and 27-28 show small tree labels beside large matrices. The labels and tree relationships remain discernible at the reviewed render size. The completed tree on page 30 is substantially larger. Actual student screens and viewing distance can vary.
- 11A and 12A page 3 use the inherited title `Breakout Room Chat`; if the activity is in person, an instructor may prefer a room-neutral title. This is a nonblocking editorial suggestion, not an assumption about delivery.
- Existing A-deck hidden weather-caption and evergreen contact/registration font advisories were reported in initial builds. Hidden content is outside the exported-page visual judgment. No actual presentation playback, interaction in Impress, live Zoom, or full test suite was performed. C and D are outside this review; the other review tracks cover them.

The scoped result is ready for the requested fresh integration review. The 12C overlay remains a known separate teaching-use limitation.

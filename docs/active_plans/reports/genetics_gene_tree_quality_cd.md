## Interpretation and scope

The acceptance question is whether students can use the visible examples to reason through UPGMA,
and then use the complete D answers with their existing worksheet. A calculation prompt needs
its inputs available while students work. Providing known branch lengths is legitimate scaffolding;
printing the requested path total before its question would be an answer leak. D explicitly tells
students to work on the existing worksheet; C has no equivalent distributed five-gene worksheet.
No new worksheet, timer, page quota, decorative perspective, or general redesign is requested.

Read the repository AGENTS and human guidance, visual rubric, voice/pedagogy/color guides,
[genetics_gene_tree_refresh_plan.md](../active/genetics_gene_tree_refresh_plan.md),
[genetics_gene_tree_math.md](genetics_gene_tree_math.md), and the fresh
[genetics_gene_tree_spec_review.md](genetics_gene_tree_spec_review.md). Inspected the C/D masters,
the complete five-gene fragment, relevant generator code and SVG declaration after the image pass
to resolve the question-input concern. This review does not establish migration equivalence to
every original slide; its rubric mode is standalone classroom quality.

## Required correction

**Q-CD-1: C pages 11, 15, 20 and 24 remove the inputs students need for their calculation.**
The three update questions occupy otherwise empty slides, with no matrix or numbers. Page 24
asks students to add root-to-tip branches but shows neither the tree nor the branches. Reading
previous pages is possible in the PDF, but during a live projected lesson this requires recall,
unannounced prior copying, or navigating away from the question. C6 says to keep the matrix,
but never establishes a five-gene worksheet or a copying step. The issue substantially weakens
the guided reasoning pauses for this visual, open-book teaching context.

Minimum correction: reuse the current native matrix on C11/C15/C20, with the update question
alongside it. Keep original cluster membership clear on C15 so the 2:1 weighting can be inferred.
Supply the already-known branch information or existing completed tree alongside C24's question;
leave the computed path totals for the later answer. Preserve C16's 20-versus-20.5 question before
C17's answer. Existing numbers, matrix styles and metric SVGs already suffice.

Source locations: `genetics/LECT11/djot/gene_tree_five_steps.djot` lines 45, 105, 174, 228.
These are generated by `genetics/LECT11/djot/gene_tree_examples.py` around lines 121 and 137;
fix the authoring recipe as needed so regeneration preserves the repair. If this shared change
also changes B/D questions, rebuild and inspect those affected pages and their immediate reveals.
Do not alter the worksheet or mathematical datasets to solve this presentation issue.

The manager confirmed during this pass that calculation inputs should remain visible. No user
decision is needed to carry out this bounded repair. After repair, a fresh reviewer should verify
the changed rendered pages in sequence, their native matrix readability, preserved answer order,
and absence of new capacity/marker defects before changing C to PASS.

## Minor advisory findings

- **C6, early notation cue:** "After a and c merge" unnecessarily previews the actual first pair
  before C9 asks students to find it. This teaches notation rather than explicitly naming the
  closest pair, so it is a minor cue, not equivalent to a full worked answer leak. Replace that
  one example with hypothetical x/y ("If x and y merge, write xy...") during the small fix batch.
  Source: `genetics/LECT11/djot/lect11c-more_gene_trees.djot:60`.
- **C17, terminology:** the arithmetic and original-gene weighting explanation are correct.
  "Equal average of the two cluster means" would be less likely than "unweighted cluster average"
  to confuse students who remember the U in UPGMA. Optional wording polish only.
- **D22:** the eight-term sum wraps onto a second line, but all terms, parentheses, denominator and
  result are readable. This is a minor local organization issue; no required correction.

D9/D13/D17/D21/D25/D29 use short prompts while students work from their existing worksheet and
tree, explicitly established on D4. They remain usable in that context. They do not independently
require a new worksheet or a second data source. A shared recipe repair may still improve them.

  20.5 and requests an explanation. C17 gives the original-pair mean and the 2:1 weighted mean.
  The result has not been printed earlier. This is the strongest conceptual checkpoint.

  large tree and C26's native three-column table show all eight final branches without overlap.
  C27/C28 distinguish fitted paths from non-ultrametric inputs with concrete unequal examples;
  C29 correctly separates the clock assumption from evidence of true history. C31 is a concise,
  scientifically sound comparison with neighbor joining, likelihood and Bayesian approaches.
- D7 preserves all 15 worksheet pairs: ab12, ac4, ad15, ae9, af18, bc10, bd13, be6, bf15, cd13,
  ce9, cf14, de11, df8, ef13. Labels remain lowercase. The original worksheet was not edited.
- D8/D12/D16/D20/D24 give all five merges at heights 2, 3, 4, 5, 7. D11/D15/D19/D23 show all
  four reduced matrices. D10/D14/D18/D22 show every required distance update from original pairs.
  The eight-term final mean is 112/8=14, so the root height is 7.
- D26 clearly shows the completed six-tip metric tree. D27 lists all ten child branches:
  a2, c2, b3, e3, d4, f4, ac3, be2, abce2, df3. Internal and tip edges remain distinguishable
  through the parent-node rows and child names. D28 supplies the root-to-tip result 7 and its
  model interpretation. D29 asks the fitted a-c and a-b paths before D30 reveals 4 and 10,
  including 2+3+2+3=10 versus the raw 12. Previous branch data are valid inputs, not an answer leak.
- All matrix questions preserve neutral cells; their answer companions use the same positions
  and pale-yellow outlined highlights. The mirrored upper-triangle highlight contains no extra
  number and does not change the lower-triangle data. Native headers are black on pale blue.
- Partial trees keep every tip visible, use the same tip positions within each example and add
  only completed joins. Horizontal extents remain proportional to height. Height labels are
  clear of the branch lines. The final large-image treatment changes display size intentionally;
  it does not change within-tree proportions or biological meaning. The SVG declares Atkinson
  Hyperlegible Next; the rendered labels are readable and consistent with the native text.
- No page has visible red REPLACE ME overlays, clipping, overlapping tree labels, unreadable
  matrix values, stretched artwork, or crowded final branch tables. A successful static review
  is evidence of these visible results, not a claim about unseen build warnings or live playback.
- Imperative prompts and direct questions generally fit the course voice. D4/D5 explicitly
  establish attempts, group comparison and discussion before answers. Separate question pages
  give the instructor control of pauses; actual adequate discussion time remains a delivery task.

# SVG composition and regeneration

The final gallery, six toolkit pilots, two trials and chromosome v2 illustration have been regenerated after the biological specification recheck. The [regeneration manifest](svg_toolkit_regeneration.json) records commands, SHA-256 hashes, output sizes and independent scratch comparisons. The gallery includes [before and after indices](../../slide-specs/illustration_examples/svg_toolkit/before_after_gallery.webp) and [four detailed comparisons](../../slide-specs/illustration_examples/svg_toolkit/before_after_details.webp), using the preserved pre-upgrade baseline.

The six isolated organelles and their labels are larger in the existing gallery grid; the composed cell retains its contents and layout. The signaling gallery and trial place the ligand's binding point on the receptor binding point, with the label close to the ligand. The text-label example retains its original three labels and wording. The Sxl trial retains italic gene symbols and upright SXL protein names. Activity and phosphorylation remain separate states.

The gallery now passes its output path to the plot example explicitly. This fixes a CLI collision in which the plot recipe treated the gallery's `--output-dir` argument as its own destination. `tests/test_svg_gallery_state.py` dispatches the real plot recipe with gallery arguments; all three tests passed.

Final regeneration used `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.12 -B` with `PYTHONPATH="$PWD"`, `MPLCONFIGDIR="$PWD/_temp/mplconfig"`, and `PANGOCAIRO_BACKEND=fc`. The manifest lists each invocation, including gallery, individual pilot modules, chromosome example, and both trial build scripts. `rsvg-convert --width 1200 --background-color white` supplied the standalone previews; WebPs use quality 90. The canonical gallery and its independent scratch build match byte-for-byte, as do all six pilots and chromosome v2 against scratch builds. Both trial builds were repeated in place with identical SVG hashes. All 26 SVGs parsed as ASCII XML and have positive viewBoxes. The 17 gallery SVGs, six pilots, and two trials preserve the baseline text-element sequences exactly; family tests and biological review address non-text data, counts, and relationships. All seven protected originals match the baseline SHA-256 hashes, including LECT02 DNA.

All 17 gallery previews were inspected individually in the rendered candidate set; updated organelles, signaling and proteins were reinspected after final library corrections. All six final pilots, both final trial previews, chromosome v2, and both comparison panels were inspected on white. Their artwork is schematic; native-slide proof and independent visual acceptance are owned by separate reviews.

# Gene Tree Plain English Rendered Quality Review

Verdict: PASS, with one minor spacing observation.

I visually inspected every individual 1200 × 750 rendered PNG: 11B pages 1-36, 11C pages 1-34, and 11D pages 1-32 (102 pages total), under `_temp/gene_tree_plain_english/`. The slides use direct, mostly simple prose; matrices, questions, tree labels, and answer text are readable at the inspected image size. The calculation prompts show the data needed on the page or in the immediately preceding example. The step reveals show the new join and highlight an actual lower-triangle distance cell. The finished and partial trees retain consistent geometry, and I did not see clipped or overlapping labels or a REPLACE ME placeholder.

Minor observation: on 11C page 32, the final bullet sits unusually close to the wrapped line of the preceding bullet. Both are readable, and this does not block the review, but a little more separation would improve consistency.

This is a visual review of the rendered page images, not a new scientific audit, a test of editable source or ODP behavior, or an assessment under actual Zoom or classroom viewing conditions. The mathematical and source-spec reviews were performed separately.

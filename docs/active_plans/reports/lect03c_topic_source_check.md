# Lecture 03C topic-by-topic source check

## Boundary

This is a provenance and coverage check, not a factual-certification review. No Lecture 03C source
is treated as authoritative: the historical ODP is incomplete, and the historical student decks
may contain incorrect explanations. The canonical single-topic file controls the question prompts;
the Fall 2025 PPTX/TXT and prior `lect03c*` files corroborate topic coverage and provide candidate
visuals. The expanded answer slides must receive independent factual review before being presented
as settled course content.

Prioritize the expanded answers for direct-to-consumer testing, mitochondrial DNA, third-generation
sequencing, CRISPR/Cas9 repair, RNA-interference therapies, GWAS, machine-learning validation, and
synthetic-biology scale-up. These are reasonable teaching summaries, but the supplied files alone
do not justify representing their detailed claims as verified facts.

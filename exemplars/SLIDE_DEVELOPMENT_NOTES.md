# Slide development notes

Sources and design decisions for the decks in this directory.

## [exemplar_06_questions_sequences.djot](exemplar_06_questions_sequences.djot)

- **Slide 1 - Now ask about one offspring.** Connect the full question to the parent-to-gamete visual explanation.
- **Slide 2 - Does every offspring qualify?** Make the incorrect shortcut a claim to investigate.
- **Slide 3 - Test the claim with a Punnett square.** Provide a large shared work surface.
- **Slide 4 - Three of the four offspring types qualify.** Answer the vote from the visible offspring, before repairing the equation.
- **Slide 5 - Adding counted the shared cell twice.** Explain the shortcut's error in the same square and positions.
- **Slide 6 - Rule card: general addition.** Generalize the correction students just explained.
- **Slide 7 - Calculate the probability of that shared cell.** Explain multiplication as a fraction of a fraction in the familiar square.

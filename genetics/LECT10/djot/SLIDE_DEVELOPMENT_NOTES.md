# Slide development notes

Sources and design decisions for the decks in this directory.

## [lect10d-chromo_aneuploid.djot](lect10d-chromo_aneuploid.djot)

- **Why Is Age a Factor?** [Tsutsumi et al. (2014)](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0096710)
  measured age-related loss of meiosis-specific cohesin subunits in human and mouse oocytes.
- **Down Syndrome and Fertility.** The revision retains the teaching contrast and historical counts.
  The original slide said "Men with Down syndrome are commonly believed to be sterile."
  [Pradhan et al. (2006)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fertnstert.2006.03.071) documents paternity in a
  man with nonmosaic Down syndrome. See also the
  [2015 primary case report](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4595396/).
